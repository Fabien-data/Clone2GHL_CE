**Subject:** Clone2GHL v1.1.0 — full visual editor before pushing to GoHighLevel, ready to submit

---

Hi [Client name],

Big release ready to go — this is the upgrade that turns Clone2GHL from a cloning tool into a full **clone → customize → publish** workflow.

Until now, users cloned a page and pushed it to GoHighLevel mostly as-is. With v1.1.0 they get a **complete visual customizer** between those two steps:

- **Click anything to edit it.** Every element on the cloned page is now editable — click it and a panel opens with exactly the right controls: text, font, colors, images (swap by URL or upload), button links and styling, spacing, borders, shadows — even per-element custom CSS. Double-click any text to type directly on the page.
- **Restructure the page.** A floating toolbar on each section lets them move it up/down, duplicate it, delete it, or insert new content — headings, paragraphs, buttons, images, spacers, dividers, two-column rows, or custom HTML.
- **Edit whole websites, not just one page.** After scanning a multi-page site there's a new **"Edit pages"** button with a page switcher — customize every page, then push the whole funnel. What lands in GoHighLevel is exactly what they styled.
- **No more lost work.** Proper undo/redo (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y) and automatic draft recovery — if the tab closes mid-edit, reopening offers to restore the unsaved changes.
- **Copyright cleanup that's actually safe.** The old version could occasionally delete legitimate text next to a © notice. The new engine removes the source site's copyright lines, ™/® symbols, logos and brand mentions with surgical precision — "Privacy Policy" links and taglines next to a notice always survive (we have automated tests proving it). There's also a one-click **Fix All** review panel.

I've attached the final package:

- **`Clone2GHL_v1.1.0_chrome.zip`** — the extension to upload (version 1.1.0).

**Uploading is the same quick ~3-minute process as before:**

1. Open your **Chrome Web Store Developer Dashboard** and select the **Clone2GHL** item.
2. Go to **Package → Upload new package** and choose the attached **`Clone2GHL_v1.1.0_chrome.zip`**.
3. Paste the **"What's new"** note (below) and click **Submit for review**.

No permission or privacy answers change — it uses exactly the same permissions as the approved version, so this should be a routine review (typically 1–3 business days). The production backend is **already updated and live**, so everything works the moment Google approves.
