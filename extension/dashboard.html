Help & Guide

Everything you need to clone, optimize, and launch funnels into GoHighLevel.

🚀 Quick Start (3 steps)
  1. Connect GoHighLevel — open Getting Started (or Settings → GoHighLevel Connection), paste your API token + Location ID, and click Test Connection. Your free trial starts automatically.
  2. Clone a page — open any web page, click the Clone2GHL icon, and hit ⚡ Clone This Page.
  3. Push to GoHighLevel — find it under My Funnels, edit if you like, then click → GHL.
🔗 Connecting GoHighLevel
You need two values from your GoHighLevel account:
  • Private Integration Token — in GHL: Settings → Integrations → Private Integrations → create a token with the Funnels scope, then copy it.
  • Location ID — in GHL: Settings → Business Profile → copy the Location ID.
Paste both into Settings → GoHighLevel Connection and click Test Connection. On success, your 30-day / 6-clone free trial activates automatically.
🧬 Cloning a page
  • Quick clone: click the Clone2GHL icon on any page, pick a niche (or leave Auto-detect), and click Clone This Page.
  • From the dashboard: My Funnels → Clone New Page, paste a URL.
  • Bulk: My Funnels → Bulk Clone, paste up to 20 URLs at once.
Every clone is saved to My Funnels, where you can review the fidelity score, edit text/images/colors, and push it to GHL.
📤 Pushing to GoHighLevel
Open a funnel in My Funnels and click → GHL. We create the funnel + page in your GHL account and upload the content.
  • Connect GoHighLevel first (see above) — this is required.
  • If the content upload only partially succeeds, you'll be offered to open the funnel in GHL or download the HTML to paste manually.
🤖 AI tools — Optimize, Logo, Video
  • AI Copy Optimizer rewrites headlines + copy for your niche.
  • Logo Generator creates a logo you can drop into a funnel.
  • Video Generation queues short promo videos.
These require an OpenAI API key (Settings) and/or a Pro or Agency plan.
💳 Plans & Activation
  • Free trial: 30 days & 6 clones once you connect GoHighLevel.
  • Bought a plan? You'll receive an activation code by email. Click the extension icon → 🎟 Activate Plan, enter your purchase email + code, and your plan unlocks instantly.
  • Upgrade anytime from Settings → Your Plan. Limits: Free trial 6 · Starter 24/mo · Pro 300/mo · Agency unlimited.
❓ FAQ
  • Do I have to connect GoHighLevel? Yes — it unlocks your trial and is where clones are pushed.
  • Where's my activation code? In your purchase confirmation email. Contact support if you can't find it.
  • Can I edit a clone before pushing? Yes — open the funnel and use the editor to change text, images, and colors.
  • My clone looks a bit different from the original? Some sites block copying or rely on heavy scripts. Re-try, or fine-tune in the editor.
  • Is my data private? Your GHL / OpenAI keys are stored locally in your browser. See our privacy policy.
  • Does my trial repeat? No — the free trial is one-time per GoHighLevel location.
🛠 Troubleshooting
  • API key / 401 errors: re-create the Private Integration Token in GHL with the Funnels scope and paste it again.
  • Location ID errors: copy the exact Location ID from GHL → Settings → Business Profile.
  • "No funnels found": create at least one funnel in GoHighLevel first, then retry the push.
  • Timeouts: check your connection; GHL can be slow — wait ~30s and retry.
  • Clone fails: reload the page and try again; some sites block automated copying.
