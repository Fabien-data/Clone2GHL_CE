Common Questions

Moving FAQ

Is your quote really binding? ▾
Yes. Once you accept our quote, that's the maximum price you pay — period. The only way it changes is if YOU add services or items not on the original inventory.
What's your damage policy? ▾
Every move includes Released Liability ($0.60/lb basic). Full Service and White Glove tiers include Full Value Protection — we repair, replace, or reimburse damaged items at full replacement value. Claims processed within 30 days.
Are packing supplies actually free? ▾
Yes. Standard supplies (small/medium/large boxes, packing paper, tape, wardrobe boxes, blankets, dollies) are 100% free. Specialty items like custom crates for art or pianos may carry a fee — quoted up front, never surprise.
How fast can you move us? ▾
For local moves, often same-day or next-day. For long-distance, typically within 5-10 business days from request. Peak season (May-September) books faster — request a quote early.
What if the move takes longer than expected? ▾
Doesn't matter. Your flat-rate quote covers the entire move regardless of duration. If our crew is slow, that's on us — not your wallet.
Are you licensed and insured? ▾
Fully. We're federally licensed (USDOT), state-licensed, fully insured for cargo and general liability, workers' comp covered, BBB A+ accredited. Our license numbers are on every contract and our website.
