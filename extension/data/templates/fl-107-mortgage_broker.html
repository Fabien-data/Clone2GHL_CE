Common Questions

Everything you need to know

Will applying hurt my credit score?▼

No — we run a soft credit pull only during pre-approval, which has zero impact on your score. A hard pull only happens once you select a final lender and lock your rate, and even then the score impact is typically just 3-5 points and recovers within 30 days.

How much down payment do I really need?▼

It depends on the loan type. VA loans require $0 down, FHA needs 3.5%, conventional starts at 3% for first-time buyers. We'll show you 5+ down payment options so you can choose what fits your savings best — including down payment assistance programs in most states.

What credit score do I need to qualify?▼

We have lenders willing to work with scores as low as 580 (FHA) and even some non-QM programs accepting 500+. The best rates kick in at 740+, but we have programs across the entire spectrum. We'll match you to the right lender for your score.

How is a broker different from a bank?▼

A bank can only offer their own loan products. A broker shops your file across 50+ wholesale lenders to find the best rate. Brokers also have access to wholesale pricing that retail banks legally cannot match — typically 0.25%-0.5% lower on the same loan.

How long until I can close?▼

Our average is 18 days from application to keys. The fastest we've ever closed was 9 days. Standard industry average is 45-47 days. Speed depends mostly on how quickly you provide documentation — we have a checklist that makes it painless.

What if I'm self-employed or have non-traditional income?▼

We specialize in this. We have bank statement loans, 1099 programs, asset-depletion loans, and DSCR loans for real estate investors. If you've been turned down by a bank, there's a very high chance we can place your loan.
