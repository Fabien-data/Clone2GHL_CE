Considerations

Common Questions.

What does a typical project investment look like?▾

Full-room projects begin at $5,500 in design fees, with furnishings typically ranging from $25,000 to $150,000+ depending on scope. Whole-home projects start at $25,000 in fees and scale with complexity. We provide a transparent fee proposal after our initial discovery call.

How long does a typical project take?▾

A single-room design typically takes 12 to 20 weeks from concept to install. Whole-home projects range from 6 to 18 months. Renovation projects depend on construction timelines, which we manage on your behalf.

Do you work outside of {{location.city}}?▾

Absolutely. Approximately 40% of our projects are out-of-state, including current work in California, Florida, Colorado, and abroad. Travel costs are included transparently in your project proposal.

What's the difference between your service tiers?▾

The Consultation is a focused day to give you clear direction. Full Room Design is end-to-end design, procurement, and install of a single space. Full Home Design encompasses multiple rooms, often paired with renovation, with comprehensive oversight throughout.

Do you handle the renovation/construction side as well?▾

We do not act as general contractors, but we partner with a vetted network of GCs and architects in major markets and oversee the design coordination throughout construction. For most clients, this is the ideal middle ground.

How do we get started?▾

Complete the inquiry form below and our studio coordinator will reach out within 48 hours to schedule a complimentary 30-minute discovery call. From there, we'll outline the right service tier and next steps.
