Common Questions

Before you ask

How many weddings do you take each year?∨

We intentionally cap at 18-22 weddings annually. This protects our editing turnaround, our energy on your day, and the deep relationship we build with every couple. Once a date is booked, that date is yours alone.

Do you travel for destination weddings?∨

Yes — we love destinations. Domestic travel is included in our Heirloom package. For international weddings, we charge travel actuals plus a flat day-rate per travel day. Italy, France, Mexico, and the Caribbean are our most-photographed destinations.

How quickly do we get our photos?∨

You'll receive a 30-image sneak peek within 48 hours of your wedding — perfect for sharing the morning after. Your full edited gallery is delivered within 6 weeks. Heirloom albums ship 4-6 weeks after gallery proofing.

Do you offer second photographers?∨

Yes. Our Signature and Heirloom packages include a second photographer (also from our in-house team — never an outsourced freelancer). The second photographer captures parallel moments — groom prep, candid guest reactions, and detail shots — while the lead stays with the bride.

Can we see a full wedding gallery?∨

Absolutely. Once we connect via the inquiry form, we'll share 2-3 full unedited galleries from recent weddings so you can see consistency from start to finish — not just the highlight reel. We believe in showing the work, not just the curated greatest hits.

What's the booking process?∨

A signed contract and 25% retainer secure your date. The remaining 75% is split into two payments — one at six months out, one two weeks before the wedding. Dates book on a first-come, first-confirmed basis. Some Saturdays book 18 months in advance.
