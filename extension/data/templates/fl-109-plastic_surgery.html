Frequently Asked

How is your practice different from others I'm considering? ⌄

Three things distinguish us: dual fellowship training at top institutions, a strictly limited patient volume that ensures every case receives personal attention from the lead surgeon, and a focus on natural results that age beautifully. We are deliberately not a high-volume practice.

How confidential is the consultation process? ⌄

Absolute confidentiality is foundational to our practice. Records are protected under HIPAA-plus standards. Many of our patients are public figures, executives, and professionals — they choose us for the same reason: discretion is non-negotiable.

What recovery time should I plan for? ⌄

Recovery varies by procedure — from one week for non-invasive treatments to 4–6 weeks for major surgical procedures. During your consultation, we provide a detailed, realistic recovery timeline tailored to your specific procedure and lifestyle.

Will my results look natural? ⌄

Natural, lasting results are the cornerstone of our work. We use techniques specifically designed to enhance — never alter beyond recognition. The greatest compliment our patients receive is that they "simply look like themselves, refreshed."

Do you offer financing options? ⌄

Yes. We partner with CareCredit and Alphaeon Credit, offering qualified patients flexible payment plans up to 24 months. Full pricing transparency is provided during your consultation — there are no surprise fees.

How do I know if I'm a good candidate? ⌄

Candidacy depends on health, goals, expectations, and emotional readiness. Some patients we counsel toward non-surgical alternatives; others we encourage to proceed when ready. Honesty about candidacy is part of our ethical obligation as physicians.
