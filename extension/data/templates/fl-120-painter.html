Common Questions

Painting FAQ

What paint brands do you use? ▾
We use premium lines exclusively — Benjamin Moore Aura, Regal Select, and Sherwin-Williams Emerald, Duration, and SuperPaint. We don't cut corners with builder-grade or contractor-grade paints. They cost more — they last 3x as long.
What does your 5-year warranty actually cover? ▾
Workmanship — peeling, blistering, cracking, or premature failure not caused by structural damage or improper care. If anything goes wrong within 5 years, we come back and fix it free. It's written into your contract, not buried in fine print.
How long will the project take? ▾
A single room is typically 1–2 days. Full interiors run 5–10 days depending on size and complexity. Exteriors are 4–7 days weather permitting. We give you a realistic schedule with the estimate — and stick to it.
Do I need to move furniture and prep? ▾
No. We move and protect everything — furniture, floors, fixtures. Smalls in the room you can declutter, but we handle all the heavy lifting and full plastic/drop-cloth protection.
Is the color consultation really free? ▾
100% free with any project booked. Our designer visits, considers your light, finishes, and existing furniture, and recommends palettes. No charge, no obligation.
Are you EPA Lead-Safe Certified? Why does it matter? ▾
Yes — fully certified. If your home was built before 1978, lead-safe practices are required by federal law to protect your family during sanding/scraping. Our certified crews use containment, HEPA filtration, and lead testing as needed.
