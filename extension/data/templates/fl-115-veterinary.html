Pet Parent FAQ

Common Questions 🐾

Is the new patient exam really free? ⌄

Absolutely! Your pet's first wellness exam (an $85 value) is 100% free for new patients. No catches, no hidden fees. Just our way of welcoming you and your fur baby to the family.

What if my pet is anxious about vet visits? ⌄

We're a Fear-Free Certified practice — that means low-stress handling, calming pheromones, treats, and patience are part of every visit. Many anxious pets become happy regulars within just 2–3 visits.

Do you handle emergencies? ⌄

Yes! We offer same-day urgent appointments during business hours, and our Premium plan members have 24/7 emergency access. For after-hours emergencies, we'll connect you with our trusted partner ER.

What kinds of pets do you treat? ⌄

Dogs and cats are our specialty, and we welcome small mammals too — rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters, and ferrets. Just mention your pet type when booking so we can prep the right resources.

Do you accept pet insurance? ⌄

Yes! We work with all major pet insurance providers including Trupanion, Healthy Paws, ASPCA, Embrace, and more. Our team helps with claims paperwork so you get reimbursed quickly.

Can I tour the clinic before booking? ⌄

Of course! We love showing off our facility. Just call us at {{location.phone}} to schedule a quick walk-through. Bring your pet too — they can sniff around and meet the team.
