Questions Answered

Frequently Asked

Will I look "overdone"? ⌄

Never. Our entire philosophy is enhancement through subtlety. Our injectors are trained to use the minimum effective dose — refined results, never overdone.

Is there downtime after treatments? ⌄

Most of our signature treatments require zero to minimal downtime. Your consultation will include a full briefing on what to expect post-treatment, including any short recovery windows.

How do I know what treatment is right for me? ⌄

That's exactly what our private consultation is for. We never recommend a treatment until we understand your goals, lifestyle, and aesthetic preferences in depth.

Are your providers medically licensed? ⌄

Without exception. All injections, lasers, and medical aesthetic procedures are performed by board-certified physicians or master-level licensed nurse injectors with extensive training.

How long do results last? ⌄

This varies by treatment. Botox typically lasts 3–4 months, fillers 6–18 months, and laser/CoolSculpting results can be permanent with proper maintenance. We'll discuss longevity during your consultation.
