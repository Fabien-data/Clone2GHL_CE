Common Questions

What our clients ask first

What does "fiduciary" actually mean?▼

A fiduciary is legally required to act in your best interest at all times. Stockbrokers and many bank "advisors" only have a "suitability" obligation — meaning a product just needs to be suitable, not optimal. As a Registered Investment Advisor, we are bound by SEC fiduciary standards on every recommendation, in writing.

How does fee-only differ from fee-based?▼

Fee-only means we are paid exclusively by clients — no commissions, no kickbacks, no product sales. Fee-based advisors charge a fee AND earn commissions, which creates conflicts of interest. We belong to NAPFA — the largest fee-only advisor organization in the US.

What's the minimum to work with you?▼

Our Plan Only engagement is designed for households between $250k-$500k in investable assets. Ongoing wealth management starts at $500k. Family Office services begin at $5M. We will not take on clients we cannot serve excellently.

How is your investment philosophy different?▼

We are evidence-based, low-cost, and tax-aware. We do not try to time markets or pick hot stocks. Our portfolios are globally diversified across 8,000+ securities, rebalanced systematically, and constantly optimized for after-tax return — which is what actually compounds.

Is the portfolio review really free?▼

Yes — the initial discovery meeting and portfolio review (a $750 value) is offered at no charge to qualified prospects. There is no obligation to engage afterward. About 30% of attendees decide we are not the right fit, and we leave them with actionable insights regardless.

How do I know my assets are safe?▼

We never hold client assets. All accounts are custodied at Charles Schwab and Fidelity — two of the largest, most regulated custodians in the world. We have only discretionary trading authority, never withdrawal authority. You see every statement directly from Schwab/Fidelity.
