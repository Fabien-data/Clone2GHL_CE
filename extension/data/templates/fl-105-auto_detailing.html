FAQ

Common Questions

Do you really come to my house? ▾
Yes — we're 100% mobile. Our self-contained units carry water, power, and pro-grade equipment. We need a flat surface roughly the size of two parking spaces. Driveways, parking lots, garages all work.
How long does a detail take? ▾
Express: ~45 minutes. Full Detail: 2.5–3 hours. Showroom Premium: 5–6 hours (paint correction is meticulous work). We'll confirm timing when you book.
Are your products safe for my paint? ▾
Absolutely. We exclusively use pH-balanced, paint-safe products from Chemical Guys, Meguiar's, Gtechniq, and Koch-Chemie. No harsh chemicals, no acid wheel cleaners.
What if I'm not happy with the result? ▾
We have a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If anything isn't right, we'll come back and fix it free — no questions, no hassle.
Do you offer monthly maintenance plans? ▾
Yes. Most clients book Express or Full Detail every 2–4 weeks at a 15% loyalty discount. Set it once, forget about your car ever being dirty again.
Can you remove pet hair / stains / smells? ▾
Definitely. Our deep-extraction process pulls pet hair, food spills, and odor at the source. Heavy cases may require an add-on, which we'll quote transparently up front.
