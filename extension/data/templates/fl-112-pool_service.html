FAQ

Pool Service Questions, Answered

Do I need to be home for service visits? ▾
No. Most clients aren't home. We need access to the pool gate (lockbox or code), and we leave a photo + chemical report after every visit so you know exactly what was done.
What if my pool turns green between visits? ▾
Call us immediately. Premium and Elite plans include free emergency re-treatment for algae blooms. Standard plans get a discounted rate. We typically clear most green pools in 48–72 hours.
Are chemicals really included in the plan price? ▾
Yes. Chlorine, pH adjusters, alkalinity buffers, stabilizer, and algaecide are all included at normal usage levels. Specialty treatments (heavy phosphate removal, metal sequestrant, etc.) are quoted transparently before applied.
Will the same technician service my pool? ▾
Yes — that's a core promise. Your pool gets the same dedicated tech every week. They learn your pool's quirks and catch issues before they become emergencies. Backup techs are cross-trained for vacations.
Can I pause service during winter? ▾
Absolutely. Many clients downshift to biweekly or pause entirely Nov–March. We'll handle winterization at the start and opening in spring (included free for Elite plans).
What if equipment breaks? Do I pay extra? ▾
Diagnostics are always free with any plan. Parts and labor are quoted transparently before any work begins. Plan members get discounts on parts (10% Premium, 20% Elite) and priority scheduling.
