Quietly Asked

The questions every bride has

How many weddings do you take per year?∨

We intentionally limit our atelier to 12-14 full-service weddings annually. This ensures every couple receives the attention, design, and presence the work demands. Once a date is contracted, it is exclusively yours.

How early should we book?∨

For Full Atelier service, we recommend 12-18 months before your wedding date. For Partial Planning, 6-9 months. For Day-Of coordination, no later than 8 weeks out. Saturday dates in peak season (May-October) often book 16+ months in advance.

Do you have a preferred vendor list?∨

We have a hand-curated network of 80+ vendors across florals, catering, photography, music, and stationery — but we never insist. Many of our couples bring vendors of their own, and we vet and integrate them with the same care. The goal is your vision, not ours.

Will we save money by hiring you?∨

The math surprises most couples. Our exclusive vendor pricing, contract negotiation, and budget oversight save our average client $22,000 — typically more than our fee. We send a transparent savings audit at the end of every engagement.

Do you plan destination weddings?∨

Yes — destinations are a signature of our practice. We've planned in Italy, France, Mexico, the Caribbean, and across the U.S. Travel and accommodations for our team are billed at actuals; we conduct a scouting trip with you 6-8 months out for venue and vendor selection.

What if our budget grows or changes?∨

Budgets evolve — that's expected. We maintain a real-time master budget and notify you of any line that approaches its threshold. Our role is to protect your priorities, not your ego — we will always tell you, kindly, when a decision threatens the larger vision.
