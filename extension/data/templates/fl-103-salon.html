Questions, Answered

How does the 25% off new client offer work? ⌄

Easy! It applies to any service of your choice on your first visit with us. Just mention it when booking — no codes, no fine print. Cannot be combined with other offers.

What if I don't like my hair? ⌄

That's why we have our love-it guarantee. If something isn't quite right, come back within 7 days and we'll adjust it — completely free. Your happiness is everything.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in? ⌄

Appointments are strongly recommended — our top stylists book out 2–3 weeks in advance. Walk-ins are accommodated when possible but never guaranteed.

How long do extensions last? ⌄

With proper care, hand-tied extensions last 6–8 months with maintenance every 8–10 weeks. We use only 100% remy human hair — premium quality that looks completely natural.

Do you work with curly or textured hair? ⌄

Absolutely! Several of our stylists specialize in curly and textured hair. Jordan and Maya are both Curl Cut™ certified. Let us know your hair type when booking.
