Common Questions

How quickly can sessions start?▾

Most families have their first session within 48 hours of completing the free assessment. We work around your schedule — evenings and weekends included.

Online or in-person — which is better?▾

Both work equally well. Our online platform includes a shared whiteboard and recorded sessions for review. In-person works great for elementary students or hands-on subjects like chemistry.

What if my child doesn't click with the tutor?▾

We swap tutors for free, no questions asked. About 8% of our students switch tutors once — the second match is the right one 97% of the time.

Do you guarantee grade improvement?▾

If after 8 sessions your student hasn't improved at least one full letter grade in the targeted subject, we'll refund the difference or continue free of charge. We're confident in our process.

How are tutors vetted?▾

Less than 4% of applicants are accepted. Every tutor passes a teaching demo, subject exam, background check, and continual quality reviews. Most have 5+ years of teaching experience.

Is there a long-term contract?▾

Never. Sessions are paid as you go or in packs, and our monthly unlimited plan is month-to-month. Cancel anytime with no fees.
