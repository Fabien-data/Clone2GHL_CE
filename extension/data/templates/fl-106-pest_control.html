Answers, Straight Up

Are your products safe for my kids and pets? ▾
Yes. We use EPA-approved botanical and low-toxicity formulas. Treated areas are safe to re-enter within 30 minutes of application. We use the same standards in our own homes.
What's actually included in the "free inspection"? ▾
A licensed technician walks your entire property — interior, exterior, attic, crawlspace — documenting every entry point, nest, and risk zone with photos. You receive a written report with recommendations. Zero obligation, zero pressure.
How fast can you come out? ▾
For emergencies (roaches, wasps, rodents) we offer same-day or next-day service. Standard inspections are usually scheduled within 24–48 hours.
What does the "pest-free guarantee" really mean? ▾
If you see treated pests return between scheduled visits, we come back and re-treat — for free. No service call charges, no hidden costs. We don't leave until they're gone.
Do I have to sign a long-term contract? ▾
Never. All our plans are month-to-month. Cancel anytime with a phone call. We earn your business one visit at a time.
Can you handle termite or bed bug infestations? ▾
Yes — both require specialized protocols which we provide. Termite treatments include monitoring and re-treatment guarantees. Bed bug treatments are heat or chemical based and include follow-up inspections.
