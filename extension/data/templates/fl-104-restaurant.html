Common Questions

Before You Visit

✿
How far in advance should I make a reservation?▾

For weeknights, 1–2 weeks ahead is typically fine. Friday and Saturday evenings book 3–4 weeks out, and holiday weekends can fill 6+ weeks in advance. For private dining or catering, please inquire as early as possible.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?▾

Absolutely. We offer vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free variations of our tasting menu with 48 hours' notice. Please mention any allergies or restrictions when booking and we'll do our best to delight you.

What is the dress code?▾

Smart casual or business attire. No athletic wear or shorts in the main dining room. We want you to feel comfortable, but also feel that you're somewhere special.

Is there a cancellation policy?▾

Reservations may be canceled or modified up to 24 hours in advance with no charge. Tasting menus and private dining have a $50 per person hold that is fully refunded with timely cancellation.

Do you offer gift cards?▾

Yes — physical and digital gift cards in any denomination. They never expire and are redeemable for dining, catering, or wine purchases. Email {{location.email}} to order.

Can I host an event with more than 30 guests?▾

For events of 30–48 guests, we can offer full restaurant buyouts on select evenings. For 50+ guests, our off-site catering program brings the experience to your venue of choice. Inquire below for details.
