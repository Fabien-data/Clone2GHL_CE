2. How to submit (about 3 minutes)

  1. Go to the Chrome Web Store Developer Dashboard (chrome.google.com/webstore/devconsole) and open Clone2GHL – Funnel Intelligence Platform.
  2. In the left menu choose Package → Upload new package, and select the attached clone2ghl-chrome.zip. It must read as version 1.0.5.
  3. Open the Store listing tab, paste the "What's new" text (Section 3) into the update notes, then click Submit for review.
Nothing else needs changing. Screenshots, description, privacy and data-use answers all stay exactly as they were approved for 1.0.4. Google usually reviews updates within 1–3 business days.
